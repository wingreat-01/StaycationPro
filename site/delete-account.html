Delete your account

StaycationPro by ProHubApps

You can ask us to permanently delete your StaycationPro account and all the data linked to it at any time, whether you use the Android app or the web app.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to staycation@prohubapps.com from the email address you use to sign in, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my StaycationPro account".
  3. We'll reply to confirm we received your request, and let you know when your account has been deleted.

Email a deletion request

If you can no longer access that email address, write to us anyway and tell us your name and mobile number on the account. We may ask a few questions to confirm it's you before deleting anything.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (name, email address, mobile number and password)
  • All places you listed as a host, including their uploaded photos
  • All your bookings — trips you requested as a guest, and reservations made for your places as a host
  • Any messages you included with booking requests

Hosts, please note: deleting your account also removes your listings and the bookings guests made for them, including upcoming stays. Please let your guests know before you request deletion.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Copies of your data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they are automatically overwritten.
  • Server security logs, which may contain your IP address, are kept by our hosting providers for a limited period and then deleted.
  • We may keep information for longer only if the law requires it, for example to resolve a dispute or respond to a legal request.

How long it takes

We'll confirm your request within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.

Only want to remove some data?

Hosts can delete a single listing at any time in the app: open My Places, tap the place, then tap the delete icon. To remove other specific information without closing your account, email us at staycation@prohubapps.com.

For more about how we handle your data, read our Privacy Policy.
